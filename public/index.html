What “edge” means

Each bet's fair probability comes from Pinnacle, the sharpest legal book in the world, with its vig removed. If Pinnacle doesn't have the line, the app uses the median of 3+ other books. Edge is what DraftKings' price pays compared with that fair probability. +2% means you'd expect to make about $2 per $100 bet over many bets.

Why not “highest chance of winning”?

A -380 favorite wins about 75% of the time, but at that price you need it to win 79% just to break even. Bets like that win most weeks and lose money over a season. The app flags them as traps.

Grades

Strong ≥ 3% edge · Lean 1–3% · Thin 0–1% (inside the noise) · Pass negative. Real NFL edges at DraftKings are usually small and rare. Some weeks nothing qualifies, and that's the app doing its job.

Stake size

Fractional Kelly: bigger edges get bigger bets, capped at your max %. Quarter Kelly with a 2% cap is deliberately conservative, because the fair probabilities are estimates.

How to know if it's working: CLV

After 30 bets your win/loss record is mostly luck. Closing line value tells you more. Log the DraftKings price right before kickoff as the “closing” odds. If you consistently got better prices than the close, you're finding real value. If not, you aren't, whatever your record says.

Caveats

Odds move quickly. Always check the live DraftKings price before betting, and skip the bet if it moved. Pinnacle and DK can update minutes apart, so an apparent edge can be a stale line. Spreads and totals only compare when both books are on the same number. Odds refresh at most every 30 minutes to save API credits. Each fresh pull costs 3 of the free 500 monthly credits, about 160 pulls a month.
